## 🏢 About Us
Founded and headquartered in Shanghai, Highjoule is a professional high-tech enterprise focusing on R&D, manufacturing, sales and after-sales service of new energy storage devices and solar power supply systems.

We stick to the development philosophy of green, efficient and safe energy utilization. We keep upgrading core technologies and product performance standards, delivering one-stop customized power solutions for global clients. Our products serve multiple scenarios including engineering construction, remote mountain areas, island power supply, emergency rescue and industrial park power distribution.
